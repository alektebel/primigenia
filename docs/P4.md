# P4 specification: social depth, Q43 loop, discovery, tiers, recipes, editing

Deepened scope and acceptance criteria for phase P4 (DESIGN.md §9, G25 and
G49–G54). It implements decisions already recorded in DESIGN.md and
`docs/COMBAT.md`. Every number marked *initial* is a starting value that the
named gate may retune; every choice DESIGN.md does not settle is listed in
section 4 and is **not** assumed to be decided. Where DESIGN.md contradicts
itself, section 4 says which reading this document used. G and W decisions win
over Q.

Sources read: `docs/DESIGN.md`, `docs/COMBAT.md`, `web/engine/profile.js`,
`web/studio/parts.json`, and (for the Q43 loop only) the numbers in
`art/reference/valle-primigenio.html` (4 species + 1 epic, `need` 2–5 songs,
DNA 6 per kill, 8 per friend, +20 for ally/dominate).

## 2. Systems

### 2.0 Shared C-core contract

All P4 rules live in the C core, next to `combat.c`; nothing is simulated in
JS. Proposed module split (names are proposals, check for collisions with the
old-stage files in `core/src/`): `social.c`, `progress.c` (DNA, discovery,
tiers, recipes, codex), and tables in `p4_tables.c`. `core/` keeps depending on
libc/libm only (Q50).

* **Tick.** The same fixed 10 Hz tick. `cp_combat_step` keeps its five steps
  unchanged; a sixth step, **stage rules**, runs after them and before the hash:
  (a) advance social skill phases and resolve completions, (b) mood drift and
  trust decay, (c) wild-raid scheduler, (d) eating and calcium intake, (e) drops
  and discoveries, (f) DNA awards, progress and recipe state, (g) event list.
* **Integers only.** Trust, mood, meters, DNA, counters and probabilities are
  integers (permille for probabilities, fixed-point where needed). No float
  enters the new rules, so native == WASM follows from the existing build flags
  (`-fno-fast-math`, `-ffp-contract=off`).
* **RNG streams (Q24: split per subsystem).** Existing: terrain, spawn, hit.
  New, each separate and each drawing a **fixed number of values per decision
  whether or not the result is used** (same discipline as the hit-variance
  draw):

| Stream | Used for |
|---|---|
| `social` | mood jitter, reaction jitter, rival heckle rolls |
| `loot` | kill-drop rolls, carcass and fossil inspection rolls |
| `raid` | raid scheduler and target choice |
| `fossil` | placement of fossil and carcass nodes at world creation (seeded from the world seed; never touches `terrain`) |

* **Hashed state (FNV-1a 64, explicit little-endian fields, as in COMBAT.md).**
  A **trailing stage block** is appended to the hash only when the stage layer
  is active, so the five existing combat goldens stay byte-identical. The block
  covers: DNA balance and DNA total, egg counter, unlocked-part bitset, per-part
  level and tier, per-part and per-move usage counters, recipe states and clue
  counters, resource meters, carried item, per-creature trust, mood, last-skill
  tick, combo state, bond latch, per-species counters and resolution, carcass
  and fossil node states, raid scheduler state, codex bitsets, and the four new
  RNG states. Padding, events and caches are excluded, as before.
* **Replay (Q24).** New log verbs append to the existing format with new verb
  numbers; existing verb numbers and golden actions do not change. The setup
  record adds the stage seed, the genome (validated) and the engine version.
* **Events** (64 per tick cap kept; if exceeded, drop the lowest-priority and
  count the drops in a hashed counter). New event types: skill result, bond
  made, bond broken, species resolved, raid warning, raid result, part found,
  fossil or carcass inspected, recipe clue, recipe known, recipe mastered, tier
  available, tier bought, part bought, editor available, edit accepted,
  stage progress full, death/egg lost.
* **ABI.** `cb_layout()` ABI version is bumped (the setup grows); the JS wrapper
  asserts the new offsets and a stale build fails on load, as for P3a.

### 2.1 Social interactions (befriending)

**Player-facing.** Wild creatures show a mood icon and a trust ring when
targeted. Five skills, each a committed move with wind-up, active and recovery
like a combat move, so they can be interrupted and can fail:

| Skill | Wind-up | Active | Recovery | Range m | Stamina | Body requirement (from `c_type`/category) | Notes |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|---|
| sing | 2 | 8 | 2 | 8.0 | 6 | any mouth part | longest range, works while the target is not hostile |
| dance | 2 | 10 | 2 | 5.0 | 10 | >= 2 legs (CP4_LEG) | moves the creature, so it needs room |
| charm | 1 | 6 | 2 | 4.0 | 4 | an eye part (CP4_EYE) | cheap, small gain, easy combos |
| pose | 4 | 8 | 2 | 10.0 | 8 | a display part (CP4_PLUME, CP4_PLATE or CP4_HORN) | the display of strength; works on temperaments 1–2 |
| gift | 2 | 1 | 3 | 2.5 | 2 | holds an item (see below) | consumes the item |

*Initial values.* A skill the body cannot perform is not in the legal-verb list.

**Preferences (G19).** Each species has a table of trust deltas per skill and per
gift class. Initial table for the valley species (names and temperaments from
the reference prototype; diets from its snouts, `snout:'herb'` = herbivore,
`'carn'` = carnivore):

| Species | Diet | Temper | sing | dance | charm | pose | Gift: plant / fruit / meat / bone |
|---|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Rondo | herb | 0 timid | +12 | +8 | +6 | -2 | +6 / +12 / -25 / -10 |
| Zanco | herb | 0 timid | +6 | +12 | +8 | +4 | +6 / +12 / -25 / -10 |
| Espinodo | carn | 1 territorial | +4 | +4 | +6 | +12 | -2 / 0 / +14 / +8 |
| Garfio | carn | 2 aggressive | -4 | +2 | +4 | +10 | -4 / 0 / +16 / +10 |

Temperaments (Q43): 0 timid (never initiates, flees at < 50 % HP when hit);
1 territorial (hostile inside 8 m + body radius unless allied); 2 aggressive
(hostile inside 28 m unless allied). Values are the prototype's.

**Trust and mood (C state per creature, integers).**

* `trust` 0..100. A creature is **bonded** at trust >= 60 (*initial*; Q-3).
* `mood` -50..+50. It drifts one point toward the species baseline every 30
  ticks, with `social`-stream jitter of 0 or 1 point, and is shifted by gifts,
  nearby raids, hunger and day/night (nocturnal species shift at dusk, W8).
* Trust gain of a completed skill = `species_delta * mood_factor * timing_factor
  * habituation / 100`, where `mood_factor` is 0 (mood <= -20, refuses), 1.0
  (-19..9), 1.25 (10..29), 1.5 (>= 30).
* **Timing.** Every creature has a `beat` (period 12 ticks, phase fixed by its
  id and world seed). A sing or dance whose active frame lands within +-2 ticks
  of the beat is "in rhythm", factor 1.5; otherwise 1.0. The ticks until the
  next beat are part of the observation.
* **Combos.** A second *different* skill started within 20 ticks of the previous
  one multiplies by 1.5, a third by 2.0. Total of timing and combo is capped at 2.0.
* **Habituation (anti-spam).** The same skill on the same creature again within
  300 ticks pays 50 %, then 25 %.
* **Rival interruption.** A species flagged `heckler` (initial: Garfio) within 6
  m of the creature being courted rolls on the `social` stream every 10 ticks of
  your active frame (*initial* 25 %); a success cancels your skill, gives the
  target mood -5 and a 20-tick stagger of your social actions. You can remove
  the rival (fight, drive off) or leave.
* **Trust decay (G19).** A creature not interacted with for 1,200 ticks loses 1
  trust per 600 ticks, floor 0. The *bond latch* (progress credit, see 2.2) is
  not lost by decay (Q-4).
* **Hostility.** A hostile (aggro) creature ignores skills. Aggro expires 180
  ticks after the last hit. A correct-diet gift thrown to a hostile creature
  within 2.5 m clears aggro and gives mood +10 (risky, since you stand in
  reach).

**Gifts and diet (G19, G26).** One carried slot: `pick #id` takes a fruit,
plant, meat chunk (from a carcass, 1 unit) or bone fragment (from a carcass).
Meat to a herbivore is an insult: trust -25, mood -15, and every same-species
creature within 20 m that sees it loses 5 trust (the witness rule, *initial*).
Wrong-diet non-meat gifts are merely weak (see table). Food gifts do not
restore hunger of the receiver in P4 (the food store is P5).

**Defending from a raid (G19, G26).** A **wild raid** is a scheduled event, not
a P5 nest raid: every 3,000 ticks plus `raid`-stream jitter of 0–1,000
(*initial*), if a non-aggressive species has >= 2 living members and the
aggressive species has >= 2 living, raiders (taken from the **existing**
population, never spawned) attack the nearest herd. Sequence: `raid_warning`
(visible as an event and a howl cue, 50 ticks), attack, end when raiders are
dead or below 40 % HP, the herd is dead, or 900 ticks pass. If the player was
within 40 m, took part (dealt or took damage from a raider, or killed one) and
at least one herd member lives: every living herd member of that species within
30 m gains +15 trust, and the player gains +10 DNA (once per raid). Raids are
time-driven so the player cannot trigger them for profit.

**Web UI.** Target reticle with a mood face and trust ring; a skill wheel on keys
1–5; a beat ring that pulses at the target's beat so rhythm is learnable; a
gift slot in the HUD; toasts for insults and bonds; a species panel (the
prototype's species list) with allied/hunted status. Strings es/en (Q48).

**Benchmark face.** Verbs: `sing #id`, `dance #id`, `charm #id`, `pose #id`,
`pick #id`, `gift #id` (gives the carried item), plus macro `befriend #id`
(approach, pick the best known skill, repeat until bonded, hostile, or
interrupted). Observation per entity adds: `species`, `diet`, `temper`, `mood`
(coarse label of four values) and `mood_n`, `trust_band` (none/low/mid/high/
bonded), `beat_in` (ticks), `hostile`, `known_likes` / `known_dislikes`
(only what the agent has already tested, so preferences are discovered by play
and recorded in the codex). Exact trust is hidden; bands are observed.
Interrupt list for macros gains (versioned, Q16): `raid_warning`,
`rival_interrupt`. RL vector: 8 nearest creatures x (relative bearing, distance,
temper one-hot, diet one-hot, mood_n, trust_band, beat_in, hostile) plus
carried-item class, skill-ready flags, and a discrete action head over
{5 skills, pick, gift} x target slot.

### 2.2 The Q43 loop

**Player-facing.** The valley holds four species (Rondo, Zanco, Espinodo,
Garfio), 4–5 individuals each, at home ranges with roam radii (prototype
data). For each species the player either **allies** (3 bonded individuals) or
**dominates** (3 killed); the first to complete closes the other path for that
species. Attacking a bonded individual breaks its bond (-1 to the species count)
and cancels an existing alliance; the pack within 45 m turns hostile (prototype
rule, kept). Progress bar = species resolved / 4 (Q-8). When full the editor
shows `advance`; `advance` is explicit (§2) and in P4 emits `stage_complete`
and ends the run (no tribe yet). The prototype's DNA-100 win and DNA-threshold
evolution choices are replaced by the shop (2.4–2.5) and the progress bar
(Q-8). Death follows G23: -1 egg, respawn at the nest as a hatchling (Q-6, Q-7);
with no eggs the stage is lost. The Coloso (epic) is **not** in P4 (Q-9).

**C state.** Per species: `bonded_latched` count, `kills`, `resolution`
(open/ally/hunted), `first_met` tick. Per creature: `trust`, `bonded` latch.
Species data (initial, replaces the prototype's `hp`/`dmg`/`spd` with combat
profiles so wild creatures run on the same combat core and counter matrix):

| Species | n | Combat profile (mouth/charge/spit/tail/guard) | HP | Speed m/s | Roam m | Hint |
|---|---:|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Rondo | 5 | 0/0/2/0/0 | 60 | 4.4 | 22 | herd, flees |
| Zanco | 4 | 0/0/2/1/0 | 70 | 5.2 | 26 | herd, fast |
| Espinodo | 5 | 2/1/0/2/1 | 100 | 4.0 | 22 | uses **spine volley** (2.6) |
| Garfio | 4 | 3/0/0/1/0 | 80 | 5.8 | 24 | fast raider, heckler |

All integer levels 0–5; wild creatures use the same `CpCombatSetup` profile
fields (plus the spines level for Espinodo). They follow the existing bot
policies (idle/random/greedy/counter) mapped from temperament: timid = flee
then counter, territorial = greedy, aggressive = counter. No special-case
branches by species id (COMBAT.md's generic-effect rule).

**Web UI.** Species list with allied/hunted marks and per-species progress
(bonded x/3, kills x/3), a progress bar, an end card on `advance`.

**Benchmark face.** `advance` is a verb, legal only at full progress; decision
caps (Q18, creature 2,500) apply; observation lists progress, per-species
status and counts; named consequence traits (Q17): `ally_<species>` and
`dominator_<species>`, carried to the next stage's start state (their effects are
designed at tribe, Q56).

### 2.3 DNA, earning and never decreasing

DNA has two meters (Q-1, see section 4 for the contradiction): `dna_total`
(lifetime earned, **only goes up**, G23) and `dna_balance` (spendable, goes down
on purchases). The score/progress side uses `dna_total`; shops use
`dna_balance`. No event, death, or edit reduces `dna_total` (the prototype's -10 on
respawn is gone).

| Source | Award (initial) | Limit (anti-farm) |
|---|---:|---|
| Kill, regular species | 6 | once per individual (no respawn in P4) |
| First bond of an individual | 8 | once per individual; breaking and rebonding pays 0 |
| Alliance completed / species dominated | +20 | once per species |
| Eating a carcass (first bite of each carcass) | 3 | once per carcass |
| Eating fruit | 1 | only if food < 80; lifetime cap 30 |
| Defending a raid | 10 | once per raid |
| Exploration: first entry to a 40 m cell | 1 | valley total <= 40 |
| Landmark first seen (W10; crater, spire) | 10 | once each |
| Fossil or old carcass inspected | 8 | once per node |
| New species first seen | 3 | once per species |
| Duplicate part found | cost/4 | converts a dead roll into DNA |

Expected valley total for a scripted full-clear run: 350–500 DNA (initial
target; the P2-style scripted bot measures it, gate in section 3).

**Web UI.** HUD shows `balance` with `total` in small type; award toasts with the
source; the codex lists lifetime sources.

**Benchmark face.** Observation: `dna_balance`, `dna_total`, last 10 awards with
source. Replays hash both.

### 2.4 Part discovery

**Rules (G37).** Parts are *discovered*, then *bought*. Discovery unlocks a
part id for purchase (it adds a bit to the unlocked set and a codex entry).
Sources and the tables (probabilities in permille on the `loot` stream):

| Source | Rule |
|---|---|
| Kill | One roll per kill: 200 permille common, 350 rare (Garfio), then a weighted pick among the victim's parts the player does not yet own. Pity (Q-10): the 4th consecutive kill of a species with undiscovered parts without a drop always drops. |
| Befriend | The species' **signature part** unlocks when the species is allied (guaranteed, slower than a kill drop, G37). |
| Carcass | Each kill leaves a carcass (2 meat units, 30 s); eating it can drop a *bone* item. Separate **old carcass nodes** placed at world creation can be inspected once for a biome-table roll. |
| Fossil | Fossil nodes placed at world creation, one roll each from a biome/depth table, rarer parts and recipe clues. P4 only has **surface** fossils reachable by walking; cliff, sunken and buried ones are P6/P8 (G37). |

Initial species part table, ids from `parts.json` (all currently `cosmetic`
with `c_type` candidates):

| Species | Signature (ally) | Drops (weight) |
|---|---|---|
| Rondo | `romo` (CP4_MOUTH_G) | `pezuna` 60, `ojo` 40 |
| Zanco | `tallo` (CP4_EYE) | `aleta` 50, `zanca` 50 |
| Espinodo | `pua` (CP4_HORN) | `cuerno` 40, `tail_hammer` 30, `espinas_dorsales` 30 (new, see 2.6) |
| Garfio | `jaw_nipper` (CP4_MOUTH_C) | `arm_grasper` 40, `cresta` 40, `fauces` 20 |

Starting inventory: parts needed for a valid body (a neutral mouth `pico`,
`pata`, `ojo`; Q-11). World guarantees (W6, checked on every seed): each part
in the tables is obtainable, every fossil/old carcass is reachable on foot, and

**Web UI.** "New part" toast with a 3D thumbnail, codex page (field journal,
G38): species (met/allied/hunted), parts (undiscovered shown as silhouettes),

**Benchmark face.** Verbs: `inspect #id` (carcass/fossil, 20 ticks), `eat #id`.
Observation lists discovered parts and a `part_found` event with its source.

### 2.5 Part tiers (G49)

A **tier** is a variant of a part family (claw -> hooked -> serrated), bought
with DNA, unlocked only after a **usage milestone** with that part. A **level**
(1–5, Q47) is the existing power axis; tier is the form axis (Q-2 asks the
owner to confirm this reading, since G49 and Q47 do not relate them).

* Genome schema becomes `cpore.creature/5` with per-part `level` (1–5) and
  `tier` (0–2); v4 documents migrate with level 1, tier 0, which projects to the
  identical combat profile as today.
* `profile.js` rule becomes `rule_value * level` instead of `rule_value * 1`
  (still integer, still clamped to 5).
* Purchase price (G37, "rises steeply"): to reach level L (2..5) the DNA cost is
  `dna_cost * {2,4,8,16}` using the part's `parts.json` cost. Tier T1 costs 60,
  T2 costs 150 (*initial*). Cost tables are data; the C validator re-derives them.
* **Usage counters** (hashed), per part id and per move: `hits_landed`,
  `kills`, `fights_won` (fight = ended with an enemy dead or fled with the
  player alive). Milestone: T1 needs 25 `hits_landed` with the move bound to
  that part on living wild creatures **and** >= 3 `fights_won` with it equipped;
  T2 needs 75 and >= 10 (*initial*). Counters only advance when the part is
  placed, so "you evolve what you use".

Tier effects are small modifiers added to the move table (data), each with a
cost, so tiers do not break the tourney. Effects apply only through the
modifier row, not by move id branches:

| Family (c_type) | Tier 1 | Tier 2 |
|---|---|---|
| CP4_MOUTH_C (bite) | hooked: bleed +2 ticks | serrated: bleed +4 ticks, bite stamina +1 |
| CP4_MOUTH_G / _O (spit) | acid: slow +3 ticks | corrosive: damage +2, wind-up +1 |
| CP4_HORN (charge) | reinforced: stun +1 tick | ram: damage +4, stamina +2 |
| CP4_TAIL (tail) | barbed: slow +3 ticks | clubbed: damage +3, wind-up +1 |
| CP4_PLATE (guard) | layered: block cost x0.9 | ridged: guard factor +0.15, speed -0.1 m/s |

**Web UI.** A tier badge on the part in the shop; a progress meter toward its
milestone ("fights won 2/3, hits 17/25"); a tier preview before purchase.

**Benchmark face.** Observation: per part `level`, `tier`, `milestone` as
`{hits: 17, need: 25, fights: 2, need: 3}`, and `tier_available` event. Verb:
`buy_tier part_id` (inside `edit`).

### 2.6 Recipes, resource meters and bone spines (G50, G51, G53)

**Recipe record (data).** A recipe has: parts (c_type, minimum level, minimum
tier), one diet condition, one resource meter (source, refill, cap, spend),
one move row, and a clue list.

| Field | Bone spines (`bone_spines`) |
|---|---|
| Parts | back spines (new `CP4_BACKSPINE`, level >= 2) **and** carnivore jaw (`CP4_MOUTH_C`, mouth level >= 2) |
| Diet condition | carnivore: the body has a `CP4_MOUTH_C` and no other mouth type removes it (derived from mouths, as `profile.js` already does for diet; no new field) |
| Meter | `calcium`, 0–100, starts 0, no decay |
| Refill | eating carcass units (5 ticks each, you stand still): +15 calcium per meat unit; old-carcass nodes give 2 units of 20 |
| Spend | 20 calcium per volley |
| Move | `spine_volley` (table below) |
| Unlock | recipe state must be `known` (clues) |

Diet is derived, not stored, and the meter is a generic table (`meter_id`,
cap, spend, source class); fuel from resin plants (fire breath, P8) is another
row of the same table.

**Recipe states (G51).** `unknown` -> `hinted` (1 clue) -> `known` (>= 2 clues,
the move verb unlocks, shown in the codex) -> `mastered` (first successful use).
Clues for bone spines (*initial*), each counted once:

1. Witness an **Espinodo firing a volley** (wild species that already use the
   move; it fires at range < 7 m with a calcium meter pre-filled to 40, so the
   hint is physical).
2. Inspect a **spined fossil** (surface fossil placed in the valley, clue text
   lists back spines, jaw, bone).
3. Inspect an **Espinodo carcass** killed by volley (quill wounds).

Without the recipe known the verb is not legal even if the parts exist (Q-12),
so discovery requires observation, which is the benchmark task.

**Combat move.** Added to `CP_COMBAT_MOVES` (level 1; the same level scaling
`damage * (1 + 0.20 (L - 1))` as every move), hitscan like spit so no projectile
entity is added (visual dart is cosmetic, derived from the event):

| Move | Wind-up | Active | Recovery | Reach m | Arc rad | Stamina | Damage | Status (ticks) | Flags / required part |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|---|
| spine_volley | 7 | 1 | 6 | 7.0 | 0.30 | 12 | 16 | bleed (8) | ranged, pierce, meter `calcium` 20 / `CP4_BACKSPINE` + recipe |

It hits up to two enemies in its cone (nearest two). Counter rows added to
`CP_COMBAT_COUNTERS` (first match wins, placed after the existing six; no
existing row changes):

| Incoming move | Defender move / phase | Result |
|---|---|---|
| spine_volley | guard / active | Pierce: deal 60 % damage, bleed applies, no riposte stagger, block cost x1. |
| bite | spine_volley / wind-up or active, gap <= 2 m | Interrupt, 125 % damage and bleed, stagger 4 ticks (same as the spit row). |
| any attack | dodge / active | existing first row, unchanged |

Spine volley has no interrupt row against charge (a 7-tick wind-up lands before
a 10-tick charge but does not stop it) and loses to a charge that closes, the
intended cycle: bite > spines > guard-holders; charge > spines at close range;
spit trades with spines at range, with spines winning on bleed and reach and
spit winning on cost and recovery. The tourney, not this paragraph, decides
(section 3). **Tuning knobs allowed** to the balance gate: damage, wind-up,
recovery, stamina, calcium cost, calcium per meat unit, pierce percentage.
**Not allowed:** new counter rows, special cases by move id, changes to existing
move rows, per-seed or per-build conditions (same rule as COMBAT.md).

Wild Espinodo use is the same move through the same table, with its `calcium`
meter initialised to 40 at spawn and refilled only by eating, so it too is
limited.

**Web UI.** A calcium bar next to stamina; a dim "spines" icon in the move bar
until the recipe is known; a codex recipe page (hinted slots shown as "?" for
missing clues); spines raise on the creature during wind-up.

**Benchmark face.** `recipes` list in the observation with `state`,
`clues_seen`, `parts_have/need`, `diet_ok`, `meter` and `meter_need`; the verb
`attack #id spine_volley` appears in legal verbs only when all conditions hold;
`recipe_clue` events carry a text hint in English (Q48). **Benchmark task
"find the recipe":** from a standard start state, reach `known` and use it once
within N decisions (N is Q-13); score is success then decisions used, reported
beside the stage metrics, never mixed into the headline (Q6).

### 2.7 Editing mid-game at the nest

**Rules.** The editor opens only (a) within 6 m of the nest marker, (b) with no
hostile creature within 20 m and no damage taken for 100 ticks, (c) not mid-move.
`edit {genome}` (Q10, Q11) is one atomic verb: C validates a `cpore.creature/5`
genome and **rejects with a readable error, never trims**: unknown or
undiscovered part ids, levels above purchased, tiers above bought, cost above
`dna_balance`, body parameters outside limits, mismatched recipe conditions.
Purchases are part of the same verb (`buy` list: `part`, `level`, `tier`), priced
in C. Replacing or removing a bought part costs nothing and refunds nothing;
bought levels and tiers belong to the part id, not its placement, so re-adding
a part keeps them (Q-5). An edit locks the creature for 20 ticks at the nest
(determinism: it is a tick event, not a pause); the human editor pauses the
real-time loop, the replay records only the edit verb at its tick.

**Web UI.** The studio (G42) opens in "nest mode": palette filtered to
discovered parts, locked parts as silhouettes with their source, price and
milestone chips, a DNA balance, validation errors from the C validator shown
inline, Apply/Cancel. The palette shows tiers and the recipes' met/unmet
conditions.

**Benchmark face.** `edit {genome}` with `buy`; the error text is returned in the
observation. Legal only when the editor is available (`editor_available` event,
already on the Q16 interrupt list as "editor unlocked").

## Addendum (2026-11-15): selection and interaction model (G75)

Clicking a creature opens a **card** (species, temperament, diet, relationship stranger → neutral → trusted → ally, species resolved count, a hint of what it likes) and a **radial menu** with four modes, each putting its abilities on keys 1–4:

| Mode | What it does | C-core verbs (agent face) |
|---|---|---|
| Attack | The P2/P3 combat moves | `attack #id <move>` |
| Befriend | Social skills with rhythm/timing (Q-17), combos, habituation; gifts matching diet; trust from defending it in a raid | `befriend #id <skill>`, `gift #id <item>` |

### Addendum (2026-11-18): select first, then choose; eating (owner feedback after the C1 playtest)

* **Select first.** A click on a creature (or its carcass) only **selects** it: the card and the radial open and nothing is ordered. Choosing a mode in the radial only changes what keys 1-4 do; nothing happens until an ability key is pressed. A click on the ground walks there and deselects; Esc deselects (a second Esc pauses). Keys 1-4 act on the selected target in the current mode (Attack: the four loadout moves; Befriend: sing, dance, charm, pose; Intimidate: roar, display; Observe: observe; Eat on a carcass: eat). `G` gives the chosen gift to the selected creature, `H` picks the next gift.
* **Eating.** `eat #id`: only on a **carcass** within 1.5 m (the player walks in first). A bite every 10 ticks (a carcass has 4 bites), +12 food per bite (meter 0..100, start 70, -1 per 60 ticks; no starvation effect yet). The first bite of each carcass pays 3 DNA (once per carcass); the last bite leaves a bone (gift item). Any other order, or a fight, stops it. Diet: the body has a diet (`CP_DIET_*`, omnivore by default, the setter `cp_stage_social_set_diet` is where the genome plugs in); a herbivore cannot eat meat (`cannot_digest`); a full meter refuses (`full`). The radial of a carcass shows **Eat** and **Observe** (a carcass holds its species' clues too). There are no food plants in the world yet, so `eat` has no plant target (deferred with the fruit/plant nodes).

## Implementation notes (C2 social slices, branch `c2/social-loop`)

What is in, where, and how it differs from the text above.

* **Code.** `core/src/stage_social.c` (all rules), hooks in `core/src/stage_world.c`, the state in `CpStageWorld.social` (`world.h`), tests in `tests/test_social.c` (`make test`), Python `bindings/python/social_smoke.py` (`make smoke`), Godot `godot/game/tests/social_flow.gd` (`make godot-social-test`, evidence with `make godot-social-evidence`).
* **Determinism.** The social state is tracked from tick 0 but **folded into the world hash only once a social verb has been accepted** (`social.used`), so every existing golden (FLAT/ANALYTIC, `world_cli` replays, the combat goldens) is byte-identical when no social verb is used. The snapshot version is 4 (the struct grew), which is why the nine curated scenarios were regenerated (their state hashes did not change, only the snapshot header and layout). Rival heckle, mood jitter and the display contest use one `social` RNG stream (seed ^ 0x534f4349), drawing a fixed number of values per decision.
* **Verbs.** `befriend #id <sing|dance|charm|pose>`, `gift #id <plant|fruit|meat|bone>`, `observe #id`, `intimidate #id`, `eat #id` (struct verbs 11-15; `move` = skill or item). They are standing orders: the player walks into range, performs, and the order ends; any other order cancels (`social_cancelled` event). Errors: -2 target, -14 in_fight (also while another fight is on), -15 refuses, -16 no_item, -17 unknown_skill_or_item, -18 staggered, -19 no_stamina, -20 busy (intimidate cooldown), -21 cannot_digest, -22 full.
* **S1 social.** Trust 0-100, bond at 60 sets the latch (`bonded`), which decay (-1 per 600 ticks after 1,200 idle) never clears; only attacking the creature breaks it. Skill timings are the table of 2.1 (sing 2/8/2, 8 m; dance 2/10/2, 5 m; charm 1/6/2, 4 m; pose 4/8/2, 10 m; gift 2/1/3, 2.5 m); stamina 6/10/4/8/2. Rhythm: the beat of a creature is `(tick + phase(id, seed)) % 12`; a sing or dance whose active frame (the tick the wind-up ends) is within 2 ticks of it pays x1.5; a different skill within 20 ticks of the previous start adds combo x1.5 then x2.0; timing x combo is capped at 2.0; the same skill again within 300 ticks pays 50 % then 25 %. Mood -50..50 drifts to 0 (one point per 30 ticks, social-stream jitter of 0-1), mood <= -20 refuses, >= 10 pays x1.25, >= 30 x1.5. Garfio is the heckler (25 % within 6 m of the courted creature, once per skill). Aggressive creatures refuse every skill until calmed (a liked gift, delta >= 10, calms for 600 ticks); territorial ones refuse while the player is inside their territory (so sing from beyond 8 m or pose from 10 m); a creature in a fight, or any fight while you are in one, gives `in_fight`. While a gift order runs the target does not start a fight, so a risky gift to a hostile creature is possible. Attacking a bonded individual breaks its bond, cancels an alliance and turns the pack within 45 m hostile for 180 ticks; hunting (an attack order) lowers the trust of the herd within 30 m by 10; meat to a herbivore is an insult (-25 trust, -15 mood, 5 trust lost by each same-species creature within 20 m). The player's body is not checked against the skills (the C profile has no legs/eyes/plume parts), so all four skills are always available: deferred until the part list is in C (D-1 below).
* **S2 observe and intimidate.** Observe keeps the player still within 40 m of the creature (or its carcass); it is "seen" (progress resets, `social_cancelled` reason `seen`) when the creature is hostile, a timid one is closer than half its sight, it is closer than 3 m, or the player moves. Every 50 unseen ticks reveals the next of three clues of that species' chain (`CP_STAGE_CHAINS`, one chain per species, authored from first principles in the style of `docs/LORE-FORMAT.md`); the third completes the chain and ORs the chain's move into `cp_stage_known_moves` (A21/A22), so it shows in `known_moves` and the loadout accepts it at the nest. The moves are Rondo -> spit, Zanco -> tail_strike, Espinodo -> charge, Garfio -> bite: ids that exist in `CP_COMBAT_MOVES`. The only lore creature file today (`lore/creatures/ecolumbre.json`) names skills that are not combat moves (`echo_sight`, `flash_code`...), so under the "skip, do not invent" rule none of its chains teaches a move; the C table is the bridge until lore creatures are playable species. A learned move whose body part is missing is *known* but cannot be used in a fight (attack needs the part level); relaxing that is part of the part-discovery slice. Intimidate: a display contest at up to 12 m, `6 x (sum of part levels) + 20 x hp ratio + d20` against `base (20/60/80 by temperament) + hp/4 + d20`; success sets the target to leave for 200 ticks (no kill, no DNA, no trust or relationship change); a 100 tick cooldown either way.
* **S3 Q43 loop and DNA.** Per species: 3 bonded individuals = **ally**, 3 kills = **hunted**; the first to complete closes the other and pays +20 DNA; breaking a bond below 3 reopens an alliance. Progress = species resolved / species present in the world. `dna_total` only rises; `dna_balance` is spent with `cp_stage_social_spend` (nothing spends yet; the shop is the parts slice). Awards: kill 6, first bond of an individual 8, alliance or domination +20, first bite of a carcass 3, first entry to each 40 m cell 1 (40 cells at most), each species first seen within 30 m 3. Not done: raid defence (+10), fruit eating, fossils, landmarks, duplicate parts (their sources do not exist yet). **Egg counter and hatchling respawn (Q-7) are not implemented**: death still respawns at the nest at full HP as before, because changing it would change every existing golden that contains a death; it needs its own golden-changing slice (TODO in `stage_social.c`). A killed creature gives 1 meat chunk (the gift source until `pick` exists); the player starts with 2 plant and 2 fruit.
* **S4 agent face.** `world-2` observation gains a top-level `social` object (DNA meters and sources, food, inventory, species progress and resolution, observe chains with revealed clues, skill table, beat length and window, the action under way), per living creature `temper, diet, relationship, trust, trust_band, bonded, mood, mood_n, beat_in, hostile, calm, leaving, refuses_social, known_likes, known_dislikes` and per carcass `meat, edible`. Exact `trust` is exposed (the owner asked for it and the Godot card draws a bar) in addition to the coarse `trust_band` the first text of 2.1 prescribes; `known_likes`/`known_dislikes` only list what has been tried. Social events: `skill, gift, bond_made, bond_broken, species_resolved, dna, observe, chain_complete, intimidate, social_cancelled, eat`. `legal_verbs` lists a social verb only if the order would be accepted now. The agentplay prompt (`tools/agentplay/policies.py`), schema checks and probes, and a scripted `friend` persona know all of it.
* **S5/S6 Godot.** See `docs/godot/UI.md` (section "C2 social UI"). The extension adds `social` and `social_events` to `CporeWorld.state()` and `CporeWorld.chains()`. The island now holds, besides Garfio (entity 1), a herd of Rondo (4), Zanco (4) and Espinodo (3) at 85-140 m from the nest (setting `herds`, on by default); until they have their own art they borrow Garfio's body at 0.6/0.7/0.9 scale.

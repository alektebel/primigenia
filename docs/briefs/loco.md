# Task: realistic locomotion from morphology (G73)
Read docs/DESIGN.md G57, G70–G73; web/creature/rig.js (shared creature builder: spine, parts, IK legs, the current distance-driven gait), web/stage/{stage.js,simulation.js} (how the C world drives position, heading and speed; render interpolation), web/studio/studio.js (Test mode uses the same gait), docs/evidence/stage/polish/README.md, web/engine/profile.js (studio genome → combat profile: speed lives in C).
Goal: creatures move like animals with their body, not with a generic cycle. Build a locomotion module (web/creature/locomotion.js) used by both studio Test mode and the stage:
1. Morphology analysis: estimate mass distribution from the genome (spine radii and lengths as volumes, parts with mass by category), centre of mass, moments, leg set (count, hip positions, segment lengths, foot type), support configuration; classify body plan (biped upright/horizontal, quadruped, hexapod+, legless/slither).
2. Gait selection and timing: dimensionless speed (Froude number Fr = v²/(g·L), leg length L) picks walk / trot / canter-gallop (quadrupeds), walk / run (bipeds), tripod (hexapods), with transitions near the measured animal values (walk→run/trot around Fr≈0.5; Alexander 1984). Duty factor, stride length and cadence from Fr and leg length; phase offsets per leg (Hildebrand gait patterns), lateral-sequence walk for quadrupeds.
3. Body dynamics: vertical centre-of-mass motion as an inverted pendulum in walks and spring-mass in running; pitch and roll from the support polygon and acceleration; heavier bodies get lower cadence and more damping; neck and tail counterbalance with lag; banking into turns; slope adaptation (body pitches with the terrain, stride shortens uphill).
4. Footstep planning on the terrain: feet land on predicted positions (no sliding: stance feet stay planted), swing arcs scale with speed and obstacle height, IK reuses the existing solver.
5. Speed realism: the visual gait must match the C speed; propose, from morphology, a maximum speed and acceleration per creature (data, documented) that the C profile could adopt later (do NOT change core/ or web/engine in this task; write the proposal in docs).
Reference: Hecker et al., "Real-time motion retargeting to highly varied user-created morphologies", SIGGRAPH 2008 (Spore's own approach); Alexander, "The gaits of bipedal and quadrupedal animals" (1984); Hildebrand gait diagrams.
Acceptance (automated, SwiftShader only): foot slide during stance < 2 cm per step; for statically-stable walks the centre of mass stays inside the support polygon > 95% of frames; gait transitions happen within ±0.15 of the target Fr; at least 6 body plans in fixtures (biped, horizontal biped, light quadruped, heavy quadruped, hexapod, long-necked tall Zancuda) with frame strips and short GIF/WebM clips in docs/evidence/locomotion/, plus gait diagrams (footfall timing plots) per plan. All existing tests (make check) pass; studio and stage still work.

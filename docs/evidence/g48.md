The original audit and first-pass results are preserved below. The second-pass
corrections and current validation are recorded at the end of this report;
[g48-after2.png](g48-after2.png) is the current comparison sheet.

24 parts, each on Merodeador (left) and Zancuda (right), at its default mount
and size, with seed eyes and IK legs retained for anatomical context. Seed
ornaments and mouths are removed to avoid hiding the audited part. Every cell
uses yaw 1.1, pitch .23, distance 11 / 10.5, at 420×300 per creature.
The after sheet uses the same ordering, cameras, lighting and seed anatomy.

Headless Chromium 158.0.8127.18 (Playwright cache build 1254), SwiftShader software WebGL only, ACES 1.05 and the
studio's golden-hour lighting. The capture harness freezes requestAnimationFrame
and renders each part/preset once. Processes run at nice 10 (inherited effective
priority 19 here). The before capture was restricted to two CPUs after startup;
subsequent captures and browser tests used two CPUs from launch. No GPU rendering or FPS claim.

## Implementation notes

The palette now has 38 procedural entries and 8 GLB entries (46 total).
All 24 GLBs retain their original SHA-256 checks. Rebuilt entries keep their
`file`, hash and source-LOD provenance as well as the required `shape_ref`;
only `source` changes. IDs, names, categories, DNA prices and C candidate types
are unchanged. `cpore.creature/4` and its validator are unchanged.

`creatorPart()` builds normalized anatomy, then scales geometry and joint
positions by `size × mountRadius / .6`. The radius comes from the sampled
anatomical spine; artificial rounded end caps are excluded, so a mouth at
`t=1` uses the head radius instead of the cap's near-zero tip. Root collars
straddle the skin surface. Body-frame wings, arms and tails reflect across
the spine; radial ornaments retain the creator's existing surface frame.
The five rebuilt leg IDs derive internal IK poses from size, local radius
and hip height, retaining their old size/rot/tilt documents and keeping feet
on the floor. These poses are never written into saved genomes.

New placements put tails at `t=.18`, the eye triad at `.92` and sacs at `.85`;
existing documents keep their saved mounts. All replacements expose size,
rotation and tilt in the panel. Thumbnails call the same procedural makers,
with wing/paddle/frill preview angles chosen to show their membrane area.
The shared membrane shader keeps the existing backlight and opacity 0.82,
with a lighter body-colour mix so sacs and sails do not become black.

visible when the matching audit view sees them edge-on. Both seeds use the

The final foot rig also mirrors the spring tendon's local geometry.

The reference HTML files under `art/reference/` were checked against hashes

## Second pass — reviewed attachment and silhouette fixes (2026-11-15)

The close review exposed problems that the original surface-root checks did
not detect. The new tests first failed on the beak collar and, after the first
correction, on Zancuda's feeding reach. The changes address those visible
failures without modifying the approved eye-triad, frill or foot makers.
Their original membrane finish and default placements are retained.

- **Beak:** upper and lower shells wrap backward from the snout tip over the
  head's rounded end, with a full-width skin collar at the end cross-section.
  The upper shell is 1.25 head radii long. Its lower jaw pivots at the collar.
  Both seeds now have a mouth sheath rather than a claw above the head.
- **Smasher:** the shoulder, upper arm, elbow and forearm use skin. Keratin is
  confined to the terminal club and three knuckle ridges. Shoulder position
  follows the widest torso node toward the upper chest. Resting reach uses
  shoulder height; the club is capped below head diameter. Regressions cover
  floor clearance and the head-size limit at size 0.5, 1 and 2.2.
- **Grasper:** the same shoulder placement and a body-relative minimum scale
  keep the arms visible on Zancuda. The elbow bends outward and forward;
  forearm height adapts to the mouth so the hands can reach it on the long-neck
  seed. Arm orientation keeps vertical independent of the local neck slope.
- **Spitter:** one centered assembly has two cheek/throat sacs, each radius
  0.6× head radius, four luminous spots and a forward nozzle. Its shared
  membrane variant mixes in the creature's lighter underside colour and
  retains the backlight shader; opacity is 0.74.
- **Wings:** the glider has one broad scalloped web, five fingers and opacity
  0.90. Insect wings have separate fore/hind sails with a visible notch, finer
  branching veins, opacity 0.48 and a view-dependent teal/violet tint. Their
  shared materials are separate from the accepted frill's material. Wings
  retain a body-relative minimum size and mount on the upper torso.
- **Tails:** new placements use `t=0`, aligned with the terminal tail tangent.
  The slender root meets the rounded cap without an inward mounting offset;
  paddle, three-barb spike and lobed hammer extend roughly 9–10 tail radii.
  Side and rear views show the continuation and distinct tips. Paddle tissue
  uses the lighter glider finish. Existing saved mounts are still respected.

Only new-part defaults change; saved `/4` genomes round-trip unchanged. No
part IDs, prices, schema, C behavior or GLB contents change.

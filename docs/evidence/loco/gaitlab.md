Result: **PASS** for Walk, Trot/Run, Gallop and Game-speed-with-Froude-gait. The "today" preset (forced walk) is measured and reported but not asserted.

## Finding: the owner's diagnosis is right, and the fix is a ladder, not a faster walk
At 5.1 m/s the Froude number of the cells is 1.8 (large long) to 14.7 (small short). Today's rule (forced walk, stride fixed at the walk law, cadence = v / stride) gives 15 Hz leg beats on the small cells and 3 Hz on the large short ones with duty 0.55 and no air time: a shuffling blur. With the Froude ladder the same speed becomes a trot/run with flight (large long), a gallop (large short, small) with 20-40 % air time, and 5 Hz strides on the smallest.

## Cells that look wrong, or limits of the model
- Fr above about 8 (small creatures at 5.1 m/s): the stride law (lambda/h = 2.3 Fr^0.3 or 1.9 Fr^0.4) outgrows leg length. The hips crouch to keep feet reachable (hip y / h down to 0.71-0.75 in the biped and quad small short-leg cells at 14.7). No slip, but it is a crouch that real animals do not have: real animals stop growing the relative stride (and use a bound or gallop with a gather phase). Port rule: cap the Fr fed to the stride law (about 6) and let cadence take the rest.
- Insects and the 8-leg: stance travel is capped at 0.9 x coxa spacing so neighbouring grounded feet never cross, so their relative stride falls below the Alexander law (flagged "stride capped"). This is a body-plan limit, not a bug; the number to port is the cap, not the law, for many-legged bodies.
- Insect/8-leg legs are seen from the side only, so the outward splay (pole vector up and out, feet 0.6-0.65 h to the side) is invisible; the knees show as up-pointing joints. There is no front-view camera yet.
- Hexapod tripod at Fr 3.2 and 5.1 m/s has duty 0.52, so there is no flight phase (cockroach bipedal sprint and bounding insects not modelled).
- Quadruped gallop spine flex, biped tail and head are cosmetic (starting values), not checked.
- Gait transitions blend all offsets and duty over one stride (circular interpolation of offsets), foot planting stays exact (stance feet never move in the world); a switch mid-stance can briefly lower the hips (reach safety). Slip during a transition is not asserted, only steady state.
- Numbers marked "starting value" in the source comment (duty curves, bob amplitudes 0.045 / 0.09 / 0.10, lift heights, lean 0.12 rad, wave lag 0.22 to 0.08, hexapod wave below Fr 0.15) are for tracker test M1 to confirm visually.

## Rules to port to `godot/locomotion/loco_gait.gd`
1. **Pick the gait from Fr = v^2 / (g h) with hysteresis +-0.04**, never from absolute speed. Biped: walk below 0.5, run above. Quadruped: walk below 0.5, trot 0.5-2.5, gallop above 2.5. Hexapod: wave below 0.15 (duty 0.78, lag 1/6), tripod above. 8-leg: metachronal always. This is the missing rule behind "walking fast". (Existing `FR_WALK_RUN`, `FR_TROT_GALLOP` already match; what is missing is the caller using Fr of the real C speed and the hero's hip height, plus run for the biped and the heavy ladder only for heavy bodies.)
2. **Duty curve, per gait** (starting values): walk `0.78 - 0.46 Fr` (clamp 0.55..0.78, Fr clamped at 0.5); trot `0.48 (Fr/0.5)^-0.25` (0.28..0.48); biped run `0.48 (Fr/0.5)^-0.3` (0.22..0.48); gallop `0.32 (Fr/2.5)^-0.3` (0.2..0.32); tripod `0.62 - 0.05 Fr` (0.52..0.62); metachronal `0.7 - 0.08 log10(Fr/0.05)` (0.55..0.75). Flight exists iff the union of the feet's stance intervals does not cover the stride: compute it from offsets and duty, do not decide it separately.
3. **Stride and cadence**: stride = 2.3 Fr^0.3 h (walk/trot/run), 1.9 Fr^0.4 h (gallop); cadence = v / stride (never a fixed cadence); walk stride uses Fr clamped at 0.5. Cap stance travel (stride x duty) at 0.9 x neighbour spacing for many-legged bodies; cap Fr in the law at about 6.
4. **Footfall offsets** (touchdown phase, fraction of stride): biped 0 / 0.5; quad walk RH 0, RF 0.25, LH 0.5, LF 0.75; trot LH = RF = 0, RH = LF = 0.5; gallop LH 0, RH 0.1, LF 0.5, RF 0.6; tripod alternate legs along each side and between sides; metachronal `i x lag + 0.5 x side`, lag 0.22 (Fr 0.05) falling as 0.22 (Fr/0.05)^-0.23 to about 0.08.
5. **Stance feet pinned, swing planned**: foot is stance when `frac(phase - offset) < duty`. At touchdown pin the foot where it is; during stance the world position never changes (the body moves over it). Swing goes from the liftoff point to `hip_x + stride*duty/2 + v * (1 - q)/cadence` along a cosine ease in x, with `lift * sin(pi s)` in y (lift 0.12 h walk, 0.2-0.25 h run). Stance travel is therefore `stride*duty`, centred under the hip.
6. **Bob phase from stance state, not a sine of time**: hip height = h (a0 + a1 W) with W = max over grounded legs of sin(pi * stance progress). Walk a0 0.965, a1 +0.045 (high at mid-stance, inverted pendulum, two bobs per stride for two-legged, per step); run/trot a0 1.03, a1 -0.09, gallop -0.10 (lowest at mid-stance, peak in flight, spring-mass). Many-legged: flat.
7. **Reach safety**: leg length 1.15 h (vertebrates) or 1.42 h (insects, feet 0.6 h out); lower the hips until every foot is within 0.985 x leg length; if the foot is still out of reach, drag it and count it. Self-check demands zero drags in steady state: a nonzero count means a gait parameter is wrong, so keep this assertion in the Godot scorecard (`foot_slip_cm`, `knee_flip_frac`, `airborne_frac` already in MOTION.md section 5).
8. **Transitions**: blend offsets (circular), duty, bob coefficients, lift and lean over one stride of phase advance (smoothstep); keep pinned stance feet untouched.
9. Roll toward the planted side (0.01-0.035 rad x (W_right - W_left)); forward lean in a run 0.12 smoothstep(0.4, 3, Fr); head counter-rotates the body pitch and roll (0.9); gallop spine flex 0.045 (Fr/2.5)^0.3 capped 0.11.

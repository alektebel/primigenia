# Primigenia: canon for the creature stage (what is already decided; do not contradict it)

Primigenia is a Spore-like evolution game with real depth. You play a lineage from a cell to space. This council writes ORIGINAL lore
for the CREATURE STAGE (no names, characters or text from existing books, films or games).

Story frame (panspermia): life arrived on this world inside a meteor: a "seed capsule" sent by long-dead "makers". The start valley's
landmark is the meteor crater. 5 meteor shards hidden on the island each carry one line of the story and lead to the crater, where the
stage ends (the creature gains tools and cognition). In space, much later, the player seeds other worlds the same way and finds the
makers' dead home world and a perfect black sphere linked to them (endgame mystery).

The island (3.6 km): a start valley with meadows; alpine slopes and cliffs; a stratovolcano (dark rock, old lava, smoke; active fire
later); coast, sea arches and islets (the epic creature lives on an islet); waterfalls and caves (a waterfall cave, a sunken cave, a
hidden cave); a giant tree and a rock spire as landmarks. Day/night, weather, seasons with migration.

Creatures (names are Spanish-flavoured and stay):
- Rondo: herbivore herd, timid, flees, spits; loves song (+12) and fruit gifts.
- Zanco: herbivore herd, fast, long legs, timid; loves dance; spits, small tail.
- Espinodo: carnivore, territorial, armoured, fires a spine volley; respects poses; likes meat and bone gifts.
- Garfio: carnivore raider, aggressive, fast, a "heckler" that interrupts other creatures' courtship; strong bite; respects poses; the
  first real enemy.
- Coloso: the epic giant on an islet; befriending giants is an owner easter egg.
- Body types seen in the creature creator: Merodeador (a prowling quadruped), Zancuda (long-necked stilt-walker), Babosa (a slug-like crawler).
- The player's own species: built in the creator; each evolution leaves the old form alive in the world as "cousins" (your ancestors).
- Variants: a nocturnal creature (night variant, bonus to befriending in the dark), rare albinos.

Systems the lore should feed: befriend (sing, dance, charm, pose, gifts by diet), observe (learn behaviour and moves), intimidate
(roar, drive off), attack; DNA; parts found on carcasses and fossils; nest, eggs, pack; a field journal (short entries).
Moves by role (Basic/Engage/Poke/Control/Defence/Signature). Deep secrets (each unlocks a move): bone spines, rock armour (eat stones on
the alpine slopes), webs/silk, ink jet (aquatic), flamethrower (volcano), thrown claws, evolved bombs, a void-adapted creature (pays off
in space). Cheap secrets: 3 fossils, a hidden cave, the nocturnal creature, albinos, viewpoints.
Two winks already placed: one fossil is a tiny, impossibly old human-like skull (nobody in the world understands it); on clear nights,
seen with night eyes, a faint line runs from the setting sun to the bright evening planet (a star-eating microbe; it matters in space).

Tone: wonder first, a bit of humour, nature documentary meets myth; short, concrete, playable. The journal voice is terse and curious.

Fixed by the art already (the planet must explain these, not change them): green plant life, a yellow-white sun, a blue sky, liquid-water
seas with tides, snow on the high peaks, one stratovolcano, day/night and seasons, creatures that walk, run, swim and (later) glide and fly.

How these were made: terrain_cli maps --layout N runs the production generator (1536 cells, 5.2 m, same stream-power, droplet and thermal erosion, rivers and sea cliffs as the shipped island) and tools/island/maps.py draws the labels. Slope colours use the central-difference slope of the final height field; walkable reach is a flood fill from the nest over cells under 51.6 degrees that are not sea or deep river, and with climbing parts (75 degrees). Routes are the cheapest plain-body walking path through each exit's waypoints. In-engine shots are the Godot game scene (software GL) with the camera parked on the final terrain.

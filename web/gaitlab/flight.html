Your choices

One star per row for each body. Click a cell to star it; click again to clear. Choices survive a reload.

Ten bodies, two pages each. Page 1: takeoff, cruise, turn, landing. Page 2: hover, glide and soar, dive, rough air and loads. Four variants per row; where a body cannot do the variant it shows the believable failure (stall, hop, tumble) instead of being blocked.

Every cell runs the same point-mass flight model as the game (lift and drag polars, actuator-disk flap thrust, stall) with a scripted pilot. The sea-green dotted line is the flight path of the body, the gold line the wingtip of the right wing, the dark disc the shadow on the ground. Each cell prints beat rate, wing loading, wing area, speed against stall speed, stroke amplitude and the measured glide ratio when the wings are not beating.

All numbers are lab starting values for the owner comparison. Nothing is selected for the game yet.

Space next body · Shift+Space previous · G other page of this body · F focus hovered cell · Esc grid · P pause · T camera (close three-quarter, chase, side, wide) · Playback changes time only. Insect wings beat too fast for a screen, so the drawn beat is slowed (label says so) and ghost wings show the stroke envelope.

Engulf: the "fauces" mouth part (A84)A creature swallows a limp, stunned creature about 55% of its size whole. Four takes: snake jaw walk, pelican gulp, heron toss, frog gulp. Swallow 4.6 to 5.3 s (A84: slow), then a belly bulge that slowly shrinks and a sluggish walk. A hit mid-swallow makes it spit the prey out.
